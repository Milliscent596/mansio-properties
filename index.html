About Mansio Properties

Mansio Properties is a Harare-based real estate company helping people buy, sell and manage property with confidence.

Whether you are buying your first home, selling a family property, investing in land or looking for someone to look after your rental, you deserve an agent who listens first and advises honestly. We take the time to understand what you need, then match you with the right property or the right plan.

From houses in established suburbs to land with commercial potential and affordable homes in high-density areas, we work across the Harare market. We stay with you through every step, from marketing and viewings to negotiation and the paperwork, so you are never left wondering what comes next.

  • Local knowledgeWe know the suburbs, the prices and what buyers are looking for.
  • Honest adviceStraight answers on price, condition and paperwork, even when it is not what you hoped to hear.
  • Direct accessYou deal with your agent personally, by call, WhatsApp or email.
